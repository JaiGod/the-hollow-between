THE HOLLOW BETWEEN

a world made of what everyone forgot

▶ Play in browser

Free · no install · keyboard, gamepad or touch · ~30–45 min · source on GitHub

You fell into the place where forgotten things go.

Lost memories, unfinished stories, people nobody thinks about anymore — they all end up in the Hollow Between. Every NPC has a secret, every enemy has a story, and every choice you make is remembered. Spare them by learning who they were… or don't, and watch the world notice.

Features

Bullet-hell defense
Steer your soul through 8 attack patterns — orbit rings, beams, mines, gravity zones and more. Grazing bullets builds Focus.
Talk, don't fight
Reason, Joke, Comfort, Intimidate or Question. Every enemy reacts differently; full pacifist runs are possible.
The Archivist
A three-phase boss that reads your kill count back to you and attacks with words from your own playthrough.
Choices that matter
Companions with loyalty, two quests, a puzzle, a secret area — and six endings decided by everything you did.
Skill trees
Guardian, Ranger, Mystic and Chronomancer. Mix freely for hybrid builds and unlock Specials.
All made in code
Pixel art is defined as text, chiptune music is synthesized at runtime. Zero external assets.

Controls

Move←↑↓→ or WASD · on-screen D-pad on phones
Confirm / talkZ or Enter
Cancel / runX or Shift (hold to run)
MenuC or Esc — status, quests, party, talents, items, settings (sound)

Progress saves at glowing lanterns (3 slots + autosave), stored in your browser.
